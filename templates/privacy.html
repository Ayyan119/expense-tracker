Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account or logging expenses, including your name, email address, password, and transaction details (amount, category, date, and description).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide, maintain, and improve Spendly's core features—helping you track, visualize, and manage your personal expenses. We do not use your financial data for advertising or marketing profiling.

3. Data Storage

Your personal account details and expense entries are stored securely. We employ standard security practices to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Third-party infrastructure providers are used strictly to maintain host environments and application services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
